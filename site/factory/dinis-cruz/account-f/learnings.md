## What I learnt
- Apple's buy pages load by script, but the price data is in the page source (`"fullPrice"`); read it with curl and a small parser. The warranty page could not be read that way.
- Apple Business Manager is now "Apple Business"; its old guide URLs return a shell. Use support.apple.com/guide/business/.
- A personal Apple Account for an agent is not what Apple's terms describe; a released device must be erased: both change what a "set-up iPad" product can be.
- riskmandate.ai's own price levels (10, 50, 500, 1,500 pounds, "one price", "no subscription") constrain any bundle: check a proposal against them.
- Relayed briefs arrive as session messages from conductor.b@; the memo on dev, committed by it, is the record of Dinis's words.
